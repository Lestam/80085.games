Dead Fools Club: 3v3 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026
Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Dead Fools Club: 3v3 game for Meta Quest (the “App”) handles, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights and choices you have. It applies to the App and to communications with us about the App. It does not apply to Meta, Photon, Amplitude or any other service, each of which has its own privacy policy.

By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • The App is an online multiplayer game. To play with other people, it connects you to them through Photon (a multiplayer service). The other players in your match see your Meta display name (with your clan tag, if you set one), your fighter and your results.
  • Voice chat: if you allow the microphone, your voice is transmitted live to the other players in your match or squad. We do not record or store voice. You can turn your microphone off and mute other players at any time.
  • We collect pseudonymous gameplay statistics (through Amplitude) tied to a random installation ID — never to your name, email, voice or Meta account.
  • Friends, invites, squads, leaderboards, purchases and player reports work through Meta’s platform, under Meta’s privacy policy.
  • We never sell your personal data, never “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising, show no ads, and use no advertising identifiers.
  • Your settings, progress, fighter look and rewards stay on your headset.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email address, postal address or phone number (unless you write to us — see section 4.8).
  • Your precise or approximate location (other than the network region your connection is routed to — see section 4.2).
  • Recordings of your voice. Voice chat is relayed live and is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Camera or passthrough images, or your likeness. The passthrough view of your room is drawn by the headset’s operating system; the App never receives camera images.
  • Hand, head or controller movement history. The App uses these in real time to let you play and does not store them.
  • Payment details — every purchase is processed entirely by Meta.
  • Advertising identifiers, your device’s hardware identifiers, contacts, photos or files.
  • Biometric data. We do not create voiceprints, face templates or any other biometric identifiers.

4. Information the App handles, and why

4.1 Your Meta account (Meta Horizon Platform SDK)

  • Entitlement check — confirming that you are allowed to use the App.
  • Meta user ID and display name — to show your name over your fighter and in match results, to highlight your own row on leaderboards, and to let your friends find and join you.
  • Friends — the names and online status of your Meta friends, to show who is online and let you invite them or join their match. The App also lists people you recently played with; their Meta user IDs (the last 20) are stored only on your headset.
  • Group presence and invites — Meta shows your friends that you are in a match they can join and delivers the invites you send. Squads use the same mechanism.
  • Leaderboards — your club points and, if you set one, your clan tag are stored on the App’s Meta leaderboard and shown to other players together with your display name.
  • Purchases — see section 4.5.
  • Player reports — see section 4.6.

Meta processes this data as an independent controller under its own terms and privacy policy. We use it only to provide these features inside the App — never for advertising or marketing.

4.2 Online matches (Photon)

Online matches run through Photon, a multiplayer network service of Exit Games GmbH. To connect you, Photon processes your device’s IP address and a user identifier supplied by the App, and routes you to one of its server regions. During a match, the App sends the other players in it — through Photon — what the game needs to show you to each other: your Meta user ID and display name (with clan tag), your fighter’s look, your badges (club member, server supporter) and daily rewards, your squad, your position and in-game actions, and the match results (frags, deaths, damage). We do not run our own game servers and do not keep this match data after the match ends.

4.3 Voice chat (Photon Voice)

  • Voice chat uses your headset’s microphone only after you grant the App the microphone permission. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your headset’s settings; the rest of the game keeps working.
  • When your microphone is on, your voice is encoded on your headset and transmitted live, through Photon Voice, to the other players in your match or squad. Neither we nor the App record, store, transcribe or analyse your voice, and voice is never sent to analytics.
  • You can mute yourself and mute any other player in the game. Please remember that other players can hear what you say; do not share personal information over voice chat.
  • Other players could record what you say with their own devices or headset recording features. We cannot control that, and we are not responsible for recordings made by other users.

4.4 Gameplay analytics (pseudonymous usage data)

To keep the game stable and see how it is played, the App sends gameplay statistics to our analytics provider, Amplitude, Inc. Each event carries:

  • a random installation ID, created the first time you launch the App and stored in the App’s own storage. It is not derived from your device hardware and is not linked to your Meta account, name or email;
  • the App version, device type, language, a session number and the time of the event;
  • gameplay events such as app start, match start and end, board and mode, match result, menu and settings actions, purchase-screen views and outcomes (never payment details), tutorial steps, and technical errors.

Your Meta user ID, display name, clan tag and voice are never sent to analytics. Amplitude may receive your IP address as part of the network connection; we do not use it to locate or identify you.

4.5 Purchases and donations

The club membership (one-time) and donations for the club’s servers (repeatable) are sold through the Meta Horizon Store and processed by Meta. We never receive your payment details. Meta tells the App what you bought so the App can unlock it. A donation earns a supporter badge that other players can see; the total you have donated is remembered only on your headset. Donations are voluntary payments for the game’s server costs; they do not buy any gameplay advantage, are not charitable contributions, and refunds are governed by the Meta Horizon Store’s policies.

4.6 Safety, moderation and player reports

You can report another player through Meta’s reporting tool. Meta delivers the report to us in an encrypted inbox; reports may include the reported player’s identifiers, the reason and any details the reporter adds. We review reports to enforce fair play and community safety and may take action such as limiting a player’s access. Meta deletes reports from that inbox after 90 days. We may keep a minimal record of actions taken for as long as needed to enforce them.

4.7 Data that stays on your headset

Settings (language, sound, vibration, hints, fog of war, voice chat), progress and experience, your fighter’s look and role, your clan tag, days in a row and daily rewards, membership and donation status, the list of people you recently played with, the analytics installation ID and queue, and tutorial progress — all stored in the App’s own storage on your headset. We cannot access this data.

4.8 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

9. Retention

  • Match data and voice relayed through Photon: only for the duration of the match; voice is not stored.
  • Analytics data: no longer than 24 months, then deleted or aggregated so that it no longer relates to an installation.
  • Leaderboard entries: until the leaderboard is reset, the App is retired, or you ask us to remove your entry.
  • Player reports: deleted by Meta after 90 days; records of enforcement actions for as long as they apply.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and up to 3 years for requests about your rights, to show we answered them.
  • Data on your headset: until you delete the App.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, other US state privacy laws, Brazil’s LGPD or similar laws), you may have the right to: know and access the personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing (including our analytics, based on legitimate interests); receive it in a portable format; withdraw consent where processing relies on it; and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to exercise them: email vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “Dead Fools Club: 3v3 — Privacy”. We will reply within 30 days (or the period your law requires, extendable where the law allows), and may need to verify your request. You may use an authorised agent where your law allows. If we decline a request, we will tell you why and how to appeal; you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Choices in the App: turn voice chat and the microphone off, mute other players, play without setting a clan tag. Uninstalling the App deletes everything stored on your headset, including the analytics installation ID, after which past analytics events can no longer be linked to you.

Pseudonymous data (GDPR Art. 11): because analytics data is tied only to a random installation ID, we may be unable to identify which events are yours unless you give us that ID. Data held by Meta (your account, friends, purchases) is managed through your Meta account settings and Meta’s privacy tools.

No sale or sharing: we do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a right to limit.
