TRRRack — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026
Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the TRRRack game for Meta Quest and iPhone (the “App”), developed and published by 80085games, collects and processes, why, who receives it, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • We do not collect any personal information about you — no name, email, address, phone number, location, voice or images. Everything we collect for ourselves is anonymous.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics (events such as “race started”, “race finished”) through Amplitude, tied only to a random installation ID — never to your name, email or Meta account.
  • To give you your game, your in-game purchases and the leaderboards, the App uses Meta’s platform services: your Meta user ID, display name and, for the friends view, which of your Meta friends have lap times. This data stays with Meta’s services and is never sent to analytics, to us or to anyone else.
  • We never sell data, never use it for advertising or marketing, and show no ads.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by email — see section 10.1.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, postal address or phone number.
  • Your location.
  • Your voice, camera or passthrough images, or your likeness. The App does not ask for microphone or camera access.
  • Head and controller movement: the App uses it in real time to let you play, and does not store or send it anywhere.
  • Your Meta user ID, display name or friends list — these are never collected by us or sent to analytics (see section 4.1).
  • Payment details — every purchase is processed entirely by Meta.

4. Data the App collects and processes, and why

4.1 Meta Horizon platform services (Meta Quest)

The App uses the Meta Horizon Platform SDK. Through it, the App receives the following data from Meta’s platform and uses it only for the purposes below:

DataWhy the App uses it
User ID — your Meta user IDTo give you your game and your in-game purchases (Meta checks that you own the App and the add-ons you bought), and to mark your own row on the leaderboards. Meta links your lap times to this ID when the App submits them.
User profile — your display name (or Meta username if you have none); the profile photo is not usedShown next to lap times on the leaderboards, so players can see whose time it is.
Friends — which of your Meta friends have lap times in the gameThe friends view of the leaderboards. Not used for anything else.
In-app purchases — your purchase records for the App’s add-ons (item code and whether it is unused)To deliver the support add-on you bought, thank you for it and mark it as used.

Your best lap time on each circuit and speed class is submitted to that circuit’s leaderboard on Meta’s platform, where other players can see it next to your display name. The data in this section is used only inside the App, is held by Meta’s services, and is never sent to analytics, to our servers or to any third party. We never use it for advertising, marketing or profiling. Meta processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Leaderboards may also be shown by Meta outside the game (for example in the Meta Horizon app), and Meta may notify you when a friend beats your time. These features are operated by Meta and follow your Meta privacy settings.

4.2 Purchases

The game is sold through the Meta Horizon Store, and Meta processes every payment. We never see your payment details. The App offers optional in-game support purchases (consumable add-ons), bought through Meta’s own checkout. Support gives no advantage in the game. The App keeps on your headset only how many times you have given support.

4.3 Data that stays on your headset

  • Game saves — which circuits are open, your best places and times, and your settings, stored in the App’s folder.
  • Recorded laps — your best time-trial lap on each circuit, stored so you can race against it.
  • A note of which lap times were already sent to the leaderboards.
  • Analytics installation ID and queue — the random ID from section 4.4 and events waiting to be sent while the headset is offline.

If Cloud Backup is turned on in your headset’s settings, Meta may back up the App’s folder to your Meta account so your progress follows you to a new headset. That backup is run by Meta; we have no access to it.

4.4 Gameplay analytics (anonymous usage data)

To keep the game stable and see where it needs improving, the App sends anonymous gameplay events to our analytics provider, Amplitude, Inc. Each event carries:

  • a random installation ID, created the first time you launch the App and stored in the App’s own folder. It is not derived from your device hardware and is not linked to your Meta account, name or email;
  • the event itself (for example: a race started or finished, the circuit, speed class, place and time, a menu opened, a purchase started and whether it succeeded — never payment details);
  • technical information: App version, device model, operating system version and language.

Your Meta user ID, display name and friends are never sent to analytics. Amplitude may receive your IP address as part of the network connection; we do not use it to locate or identify you.

5. What we never do with data

  • We do not sell or rent data.
  • We do not use any data — including Meta platform data — for advertising, marketing or profiling.
  • We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We do not show ads in the App.

7. International data transfers

Amplitude may process analytics data in the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Data handled by Meta’s platform is processed by Meta under its own terms.

8. Retention

Analytics data is kept for no longer than 24 months, then deleted or aggregated so that it no longer relates to an installation. Data on your headset stays there until you delete the App. Your leaderboard entries stay on Meta’s platform until they are replaced by a better time, deleted at your request, or the leaderboard is reset or closed.

9. Security

Analytics events are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The App talks to Meta’s platform only through Meta’s own SDK. We keep the data we handle to the minimum described above.

10. Your rights

Every player, wherever they live, may ask us to access, correct or delete their data, to restrict or object to its processing (including our analytics), and to receive a copy of it. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the EEA/UK: your local data protection authority).

10.1 How to request deletion of your data

  • By email: write to vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “TRRRack — Delete my data”. Include your Meta display name to have your leaderboard entries deleted, and your installation ID if you want specific analytics events deleted. We will delete the data and confirm by email within 30 days.
  • On your headset: uninstalling the App erases your saves, settings, recorded laps and the installation ID, after which past analytics events can no longer be linked to you.

Anonymous data (GDPR Art. 11): because analytics data is tied only to a random installation ID, we may be unable to identify which events are yours unless you give us that ID. This right is available to every player, regardless of location.

11. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. The App on iPhone

TRRRack is also available for iPhone. On iPhone the App works as described above, with these differences. Where this section differs from sections 1–13, this section applies to the iPhone version.

  • No Meta account and no leaderboards. The iPhone version does not use the Meta Horizon Platform: there is no entitlement check, no Meta user ID, no Meta friends and no online leaderboards (section 4.1 does not apply). The Records screen shows only your own best results, kept on your iPhone.
  • Purchases. The App is free to download. The club membership (a one-time purchase that unlocks all craft liveries) and the optional support purchases are in-app purchases made through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. The App receives only Apple’s confirmation that an item was bought (or restored) and remembers on your iPhone that you are a member and how many times you have given support. Support gives no advantage in the game.
  • Touch, tilt and game controllers. The App reads your touches, the iPhone’s motion sensor (only when you choose tilt steering) and a connected game controller in real time to let you play, and does not store or send this anywhere. The App does not ask for microphone, camera, location or contacts access.
  • Data that stays on your iPhone. Game saves, settings, recorded laps and the membership status are stored in the App’s own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the App deletes them.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. The iPhone version sends no analytics (section 4.4 does not apply to it), shows no ads, has no servers of its own, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Service provider on iPhone: Apple (App Store distribution and payments — an independent controller under Apple’s terms and privacy policy).
