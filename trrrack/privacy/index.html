TRRRack — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026
Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the TRRRack game for Meta Quest (the “App”), developed and published by 80085games, handles, why, and what choices you have. The App collects as little as possible: it has no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • The App sends your best lap times to the game’s leaderboards, which are run by Meta’s platform.
  • To show the leaderboards, the App reads your Meta user ID, your display name and, for the friends view, which of your Meta friends have times in the game.
  • We do not run analytics, show ads, or operate any server that receives your data.
  • We never use your data for advertising or marketing, never sell it, and never share it with advertising networks.
  • Your game saves, settings and recorded laps stay on your headset.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, postal address or phone number.
  • Your location.
  • Your voice, camera or passthrough images, or your likeness. The App does not ask for microphone or camera access.
  • Head and controller movement: the App uses it in real time to let you play, and does not store or send it anywhere.
  • Gameplay analytics or usage statistics of any kind.
  • Payment details — the purchase of the game is processed entirely by Meta.

4. Information the App handles, and why

4.1 Meta Horizon platform features

The App uses the Meta Horizon Platform SDK for:

  • Entitlement check — confirming that you own the game;
  • Leaderboards — your best lap time on each circuit and speed class is submitted to that circuit’s leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your display name. To display a leaderboard, the App reads the Meta user ID and display name of the players in it: the name is shown next to each time, and your own ID is used only to mark your own row;
  • Friends view — the App asks Meta for the leaderboard filtered to your Meta friends, so you can compare lap times with them. The App does not read your friends list for any other purpose.

The App does not store this data anywhere else and does not send it to us or to any third party. Meta processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. We use it only to provide these features inside the game — never for advertising or marketing.

Leaderboards may also be shown by Meta outside the game (for example in the Meta Horizon app), and Meta may notify you when a friend beats your time. These features are operated by Meta and follow your Meta privacy settings.

4.2 Purchases

The game is sold through the Meta Horizon Store, and Meta processes every payment. We never see your payment details. The App offers optional in-game support purchases (consumable add-ons). They are bought through Meta’s own checkout: Meta processes the payment, and the App only receives the item’s code to thank you and to mark the purchase as used. Support gives no advantage in the game. The App keeps on your headset only how many times you have given support.

4.3 Data that stays on your headset

  • Game saves — which circuits are open, your best places and times, and your settings, stored in the App’s folder.
  • Recorded laps — your best time-trial lap on each circuit, stored so you can race against it.
  • A note of which lap times were already sent to the leaderboards.

If Cloud Backup is turned on in your headset’s settings, Meta may back up the App’s folder to your Meta account so your progress follows you to a new headset. That backup is run by Meta; we have no access to it.

5. What we never do with data

  • We do not sell or rent data.
  • We do not use any data — including Meta platform data — for advertising, marketing or profiling.
  • We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We do not show ads in the App.

7. International data transfers

We do not transfer your data anywhere ourselves. Data handled by Meta’s platform is processed by Meta under its own terms.

8. Retention

Data on your headset stays there until you delete the App. Your leaderboard entries stay on Meta’s platform until they are replaced by a better time, deleted at your request, or the leaderboard is reset or closed.

9. Security

The App talks only to Meta’s platform, through Meta’s own SDK. We hold no copy of your data on any server of ours.

10. Your rights

Every player, wherever they live, may ask us to access, correct or delete their data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the EEA/UK: your local data protection authority).

Deleting your data. Uninstalling the App erases your saves, settings and recorded laps from the headset. To have your leaderboard entries deleted, write to us at the address below, mention TRRRack and your Meta display name, and we will delete them within 30 days. This right is available to every player, regardless of location.

11. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
