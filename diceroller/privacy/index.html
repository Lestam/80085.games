Dice Roller: d20 & Tarot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026 (tips added)

This Privacy Policy describes how Vladimir Matsel, publishing as 80085games (“we”, “us”), handles information in connection with the Dice Roller: d20 & Tarot application for iPhone and Apple Watch (the “App”).

1. Summary

  • We collect no data. No personal information, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.
  • The App works fully offline and makes no network connections.
  • The App contains no third-party SDKs and no ads.
  • Optional tips are paid through Apple's In-App Purchase; we never see your payment details.
  • Your settings stay on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

WhatWhyWhere
Selected mode, dice colour, “shake to roll” switchSo the App opens the way you left itOnly on your iPhone / Apple Watch (app settings storage). Never sent to us or anyone else.

Roll results are not saved; there is no history.

3. Motion sensors

The App reads the device's motion sensors to detect a shake (when you turn on “shake to roll”) and to tilt the glossy highlight on the dice. These readings are processed instantly on the device, are not stored and are never transmitted.

4. Optional tips

After every tenth roll the App offers optional tips (In-App Purchases). Payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We receive only anonymous, aggregated sales reports from Apple — never your name, Apple ID, email or payment details. Tips unlock nothing and are never required.

5. Sharing

Because we collect nothing, we share nothing. We do not sell, rent or disclose any data.

6. Children

The App collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Apple

Downloads and updates are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Apple.

8. Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before that version is released.
