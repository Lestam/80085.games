GoGoGo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the GoGoGo game for Meta Quest (the “App”) handles, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights you have. It does not apply to Meta, Photon, Amplitude or any other service, each of which has its own privacy policy.

By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • GoGoGo teaches and plays the board game Go: lessons with a voiced teacher, Go problems, games against the computer, and games online, one on one, against a friend or another player.
  • Online games connect you to the other player through Photon (a multiplayer service). Only the game itself travels — the board size and your moves — not your Meta name or account.
  • To invite a friend, the App uses Meta’s own presence and invite features; that data stays with Meta.
  • There is no voice chat, no text chat and no advertising. The App does not use the microphone or the cameras’ images.
  • We collect pseudonymous gameplay statistics (through Amplitude) tied to a random installation ID — never to your name, email or Meta account.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your headset. We never sell personal data.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email, postal address or phone number (unless you write to us — section 4.6).
  • Your Meta user ID, display name, profile or friends list — the App uses some of them only through Meta’s services (section 4.1) and never sends them to us, to Photon or to analytics.
  • Your voice or microphone audio — the App does not use the microphone. The teacher’s voice is pre-recorded and plays from the App.
  • Camera or passthrough images, or your likeness. In mixed reality the App asks Meta for the position of your table (scene data) to place the board on it; that data stays on your headset.
  • Hand, head or controller movement history (used in real time only, never stored).
  • Your location (other than the network region your connection is routed to — section 4.2).
  • Payment details — every purchase is processed entirely by Meta.
  • Advertising identifiers, hardware identifiers, contacts, photos or files.

4. Information the App handles, and why

4.1 Meta Horizon Platform

DataWhy the App uses it
User ID — your Meta user IDTo give you your game: Meta checks that you own the App (entitlement check). To unlock an achievement on your profile when you finish the first chapter of lessons. To show your presence while your online game waits for a friend, and to send and receive invites.

This data is used only inside the App and is held by Meta’s services; it is never sent to us, to Photon or to analytics. Meta processes it as an independent controller under its own terms and privacy policy.

4.2 Online games (Photon)

Online games run through Photon, a multiplayer network service of Exit Games, Inc. (servers in the EU region). To connect you, Photon processes your device’s IP address and a random identifier that Photon creates for the connection. During a game the App exchanges with the other player, through Photon, only what the game needs: a random room name, the board size and your moves (stones, passes, resigning, a rematch). We do not run our own game servers and do not keep game data after the game ends.

4.3 Gameplay analytics (pseudonymous usage data)

To keep the game stable and see where players get stuck, the App sends gameplay statistics to our analytics provider, Amplitude, Inc. Each event carries:

  • a random installation ID, created the first time you launch the App and stored in the App’s own storage. It is not derived from your device hardware and is not linked to your Meta account, name or email;
  • the App version, device type, language, a session number and the time of the event;
  • gameplay events such as app start, game start and end (board size, computer level, result, number of moves), lessons and problems started and finished, hints and undos, how stones were placed (tap or pinch), buttons pressed, mixed reality on or off, online games started and finished, and technical errors.

Your Meta account and identity are never sent to analytics. Amplitude may receive your IP address as part of the network connection; we do not use it to locate or identify you.

4.4 Purchases

The game is sold through the Meta Horizon Store, and Meta processes every payment. We never see your payment details.

4.5 Data that stays on your headset

Settings (language, input mode), your progress, and the analytics installation ID and event queue are stored in the App’s own storage on your headset. We cannot access this data. If Cloud Backup is turned on in your headset’s settings, Meta may back up the App’s storage to your Meta account; that backup is run by Meta and we have no access to it.

4.6 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

Online games, entitlement, achievements and invites: performance of our contract with you — providing the game you chose to play (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Gameplay analytics: our legitimate interests in keeping the game working and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f)), assessed as low-risk because the data is pseudonymous, minimal and never used for marketing; you may object — see section 10. Answering your messages: legitimate interests, and legal obligation where a request concerns your rights (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Where personal data is transferred from the EEA, UK or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer relies on appropriate safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers’ data processing terms.

9. Retention

  • Game data relayed through Photon: only for the duration of the game.
  • Analytics data: no longer than 24 months, then deleted or aggregated so that it no longer relates to an installation.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and up to 3 years for requests about your rights.
  • Data on your headset: until you delete the App.

10. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you have the right to know and access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, other US state privacy laws or Brazil’s LGPD may give you further rights, such as lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How: email vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “GoGoGo — Privacy”. We will reply within 30 days (or the period your law requires). Analytics data is tied only to a random installation ID, so we may be unable to identify which events are yours unless you give us that ID (GDPR Art. 11); the App shows it under ABOUT. Data held by Meta is managed through your Meta account settings. Uninstalling the App deletes everything stored on your headset, including the analytics installation ID, after which past analytics events can no longer be linked to you.

11. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Connections to Photon are encrypted by Photon’s service; analytics data is sent over HTTPS; we keep no personal data on servers of our own. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep what we handle to a minimum.
