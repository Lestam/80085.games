Tank Mania (Meta Quest) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026
Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Tank Mania game for Meta Quest (the “App”) handles, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights and choices you have. It applies to the Meta Quest version of the App, distributed through the Meta Horizon Store, and to communications with us about it. Versions of the App on other platforms have their own privacy policies. This Policy does not apply to Meta, Photon or any other service, each of which has its own privacy policy. It replaces the earlier privacy policy published for the App.

By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • The App is an online multiplayer tank game. To play with other people, it connects you to them through Photon (a multiplayer service). The other players in your match see your Meta display name above your tank, and your tank’s actions.
  • Voice chat: if you allow the microphone, your voice is transmitted live to the other players in your match. We do not record or store voice.
  • We use your Meta account data only to run the game — never for advertising, marketing or any other purpose.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising services, show no ads, and never sell your personal data.
  • We do not run our own servers and do not keep match data after a match ends.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email address, postal address or phone number (unless you write to us — see section 4.4).
  • Your precise or approximate location (other than the network region your connection is routed to — see section 4.2).
  • Recordings of your voice. Voice chat is relayed live and is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Camera or passthrough images, or your likeness.
  • Hand, head or controller movement history. The App uses these in real time to let you play and does not store them.
  • Your Meta friends list, profile photo, payment details, advertising identifiers, contacts, photos or files.
  • Biometric data. We do not create voiceprints, face templates or any other biometric identifiers.

4. Information the App handles, and why

4.1 Your Meta account (Meta Horizon Platform SDK)

  • Meta user ID — to identify you as a player in online matches.
  • Meta display name — to show your name above your tank so other players in the match can see who they are playing against.

Meta processes this data as an independent controller under its own terms and privacy policy. We use it only to provide these features inside the App — never for advertising or marketing, and we do not pass it to anyone except Photon for running the match (section 4.2).

4.2 Online matches (Photon)

Online matches run through Photon, a multiplayer network service of Exit Games, Inc. To connect you, Photon processes your device’s IP address and a user identifier supplied by the App, and routes you to one of its server regions. During a match, the App sends the other players in it — through Photon — what the game needs to show you to each other: your Meta display name, your tank, its position and in-game actions, and the match results. Players of the App on other platforms may join the same matches and see the same information. We do not run our own game servers and do not keep this match data after the match ends.

4.3 Voice chat (Photon Voice)

  • Voice chat uses your headset’s microphone only after you grant the App the microphone permission. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your headset’s settings.
  • When your microphone is on, your voice is transmitted live, through Photon Voice, to the other players in your match. Neither we nor the App record, store, transcribe or analyse your voice.
  • Please remember that other players can hear what you say; do not share personal information over voice chat. Other players could record what you say with their own devices; we cannot control that.

4.4 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

10. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you have the right to: know and access the personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; receive it in a portable format; and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. These rights are available to every player, regardless of country or region. Laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, other US state privacy laws or Brazil’s LGPD may give you further rights, such as lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to exercise them: email vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “Tank Mania — Privacy”. We will reply within 30 days (or the period your law requires, extendable where the law allows), and may need to verify your request. If we decline a request, we will tell you why and how to appeal; you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Choices in the App: you can turn voice chat off by withdrawing the microphone permission. Uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored on your headset. Data held by Meta (your account) is managed through your Meta account settings and Meta’s privacy tools.

No sale or sharing: we do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country to consent to the processing of personal data online), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Online play and voice chat let players communicate with people they do not know; parents and guardians can use Meta’s parental supervision tools to manage this. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the competent authorities where the law requires.
