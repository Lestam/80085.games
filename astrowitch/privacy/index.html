AstroWitch (Meta Quest) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026
Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the AstroWitch app for Meta Quest (the “App”), developed and published by 80085games, collects and processes, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights and choices you have. It applies to the App as distributed through the Meta Horizon Store, and to communications with us about it. It does not apply to Meta, Google, Cloudflare or any other service, each of which has its own privacy policy.

By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • AstroWitch is a voice conversation with an AI fortune teller, Lupita. Her voice and replies are generated by Google’s Gemini AI service. When you talk to her, your voice is streamed live to Google so the AI can understand and answer you (section 4.1).
  • For astrology readings you tell Lupita your date, time and place of birth. The App calculates the readings on your headset and keeps your birth details only on your headset (section 4.2).
  • Our small server checks with Meta that the App is genuine, gives the headset a short-lived key for the AI service, and keeps a pseudonymous record of your free-reading limits and purchases (section 4.4). It never receives your voice.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Meta. We never see your card or payment details (section 4.5).
  • We do not use analytics or advertising services, show no ads, and never sell your personal data.
  • The App is for adults (18+) and is entertainment: nothing Lupita says is advice of any kind (section 13).
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by email — see section 12.1.

4. Information we collect and process, and why

4.1 Talking to Lupita (Google Gemini)

  • Lupita listens through your headset’s microphone, only after you grant the App the microphone permission. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your headset’s settings; without it you can still use the buttons on the table, but Lupita cannot hear you.
  • While a conversation is open, the App streams your voice directly from your headset to the Gemini API of Google LLC, together with the facts of your reading that the App calculated (for example, the cards you drew or the positions of the planets at your birth). Gemini turns your speech into text, generates Lupita’s answer and speaks it. The App also receives a text transcript of the conversation, which it uses only in the moment — to show it and to check it for the safety topics described in section 13 — and does not store.
  • Google processes this data as our service provider, under its terms for paid services. Under those terms Google does not use your conversations to improve its products, and logs prompts and responses only for a limited period, solely to detect and prevent abuse and violations of its policies and for legally required disclosures. See Google’s Gemini API Additional Terms and Privacy Policy.
  • Please do not tell Lupita sensitive information — for example about your health, finances, other people, or anything you would not want an AI service to process. You do not need to tell her your real name.

4.2 Your birth details (stored on your headset)

To calculate your zodiac sign, ascendant and forecasts, Lupita asks for your date of birth and, optionally, your time and place of birth. You say them aloud (so they pass through Gemini as described in section 4.1) and confirm them on a brass plate on the table. The App then:

  • looks up the place in a list of cities built into the App to find its coordinates and time zone — on your headset, without sending it anywhere;
  • calculates the astrology on your headset;
  • stores your birth details only on your headset, so that Lupita does not have to ask again. We cannot access them. They are deleted when you uninstall the App.

When you ask for a reading, the App gives Gemini the calculated facts of that reading (and, where the reading needs it, your birth details) so Lupita can interpret them. Details of paid readings are given to Gemini only after you have bought them.

4.5 Free readings and purchases

Some readings are free: your zodiac portrait, a short forecast for the year, a short ascendant reading and the first part of a compatibility reading, and tarot, runes and the I Ching once a week each. Full readings and extra conversation time are optional in-app purchases, sold through the Meta Horizon Store checkout. Meta processes every payment as an independent controller; we receive only which item was bought and a purchase identifier (section 4.3), never your card or payment details. Refunds are governed by the Meta Horizon Store’s policies.

4.6 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

4.7 Data that stays on your headset

Your birth details (section 4.2), the date you accepted the start-up notice, and your settings are stored in the App’s own folder on your headset. We cannot access this data. The App does not allow this folder to be backed up to your Meta account (Cloud Backup), so it stays on the headset and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

12.1 How to request deletion of your data

  • By email: write to vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “AstroWitch — Delete my data”. We will delete the usage counters and purchase records held under your pseudonymous key and confirm by email within 30 days. Because we do not store your Meta user ID, we may ask you to open the App once so that we can match your request to your key. Deleting purchase records means the App may no longer restore items you bought.
  • On your headset: uninstalling the App deletes your birth details and everything else the App stored there.
  • Data held by Google about your use of the AI service is deleted under Google’s terms (section 4.1); data held by Meta is managed through your Meta account settings and Meta’s privacy tools.

Choices in the App: you can withdraw the microphone permission; you do not have to give your birth time or place (Lupita can still read your sign from the date alone); and you can choose readings with the buttons on the table.

Pseudonymous data (GDPR Art. 11): because our server keeps only a pseudonymous key, we may be unable to identify which records are yours unless you help us match your request (section 12.1).

No sale or sharing: we do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a right to limit.

13. Adults only, safety, and entertainment

The App is intended only for adults aged 18 or over. It is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has used the App or provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

AstroWitch is an attraction for entertainment. Nothing Lupita says is medical, psychological, legal, financial or any other professional advice, a recommendation or a call to action. If a conversation turns to self-harm or explicit sexual content, Lupita steers away from it and the App shows information on where to find help (for example, findahelpline.com); this check runs in the moment on your headset, on the conversation transcript, and nothing is stored or reported to us.

We keep the personal data we handle to the minimum described above. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the competent authorities where the law requires.
