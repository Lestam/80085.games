VALXR — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the VALXR game for Meta Quest (the “App”), developed and published by 80085games, collects and processes, why, who receives it, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • We do not collect any personal information on our own servers — we have none. The App has no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no online play.
  • The App uses Meta’s platform services only to check that you own it and to record an achievement. This data stays with Meta’s services.
  • Your progress is saved on your headset only.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by email — see section 9.1.

2. Who is responsible (data controller)

80085games (developer and publisher of VALXR)
Vladimir Matsel, individual developer
Contact: vnmtsl@gmail.com

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, postal address or phone number.
  • Your location.
  • Your voice, camera or passthrough images, room or spatial data, or your likeness.
  • Head and controller movement: the App uses it in real time to let you play, and does not store or send it anywhere.
  • Usage statistics or analytics of any kind.
  • Payment details — the purchase is processed entirely by Meta.

4. Data the App processes, and why

4.1 Meta Horizon platform services

DataWhy the App uses it
User ID — your Meta user IDTo give you your game: Meta checks that you own the App (entitlement check). To unlock the “Touch Grass” achievement on your profile when you clear your first stage.

This data is used only inside the App and is held by Meta’s services; it is never sent to us. We never use it for advertising, marketing or profiling. Meta processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

4.2 Purchases

The game is sold through the Meta Horizon Store, and Meta processes every payment. We never see your payment details.

4.3 Data that stays on your headset

  • Game progress — silver, Workshop upgrades, unlocked heroes and stages, quests and records.
  • Settings — chosen hero, stage, tier and table position.

This data is stored in the App’s own folder and is never sent to us. If Cloud Backup is turned on in your headset’s settings, Meta may back up the App’s folder to your Meta account; that backup is run by Meta and we have no access to it.

5. What we never do with data

We never sell data, never share it, never use it for advertising or marketing, and show no ads.

6. Service providers

  • Meta Platforms runs the store, the purchase, the entitlement check and achievements under its own privacy policy.

7. Retention

We hold no data about you. Data on your headset is kept until you uninstall the App. Meta keeps its platform data under its own policy.

8. Security

Nothing leaves your headset except Meta’s own platform calls, over Meta’s secure connections.

9. Your rights

Every player, wherever they live, may ask us to access, correct or delete their data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the EEA/UK: your local data protection authority).

9.1 How to request deletion of your data

  • On your headset: uninstalling the App erases everything it stored.
  • By email: write to vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “VALXR — Delete my data”. We will answer and confirm within 30 days. Data held by Meta (your achievement) can also be managed in your Meta account settings.

This right is available to every player, regardless of location.

10. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this Policy

If we change this Policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
