Dead Fools Club: Headshot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Dead Fools Club: Headshot game for Meta Quest (the “App”) handles, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights and choices you have. It applies to the App and to communications with us about the App. It does not apply to Meta, Photon or any other service, each of which has its own privacy policy.

By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • The App is a VR shooter you can play against bots or online against other people. Online matches connect you to other players through Photon (a multiplayer service). Other players in your match see your fighter, its movements and its number on the ladder — not your Meta name or ID.
  • The App collects and processes data from Meta’s platform: your Meta user ID and display name, your friends, invites, the deep link of a match you were invited to, leaderboard entries and your in-app purchase records (section 4.1). It uses them only inside the App, for the features listed.
  • There is no voice or text chat, no analytics and no ads. We use no advertising identifiers, and we never sell or “share” your personal data.
  • The App is free. The optional club membership and donations for the servers are bought through Meta’s own checkout; Meta processes the payment.
  • Your settings, ladder place and fighter look stay on your headset.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email address, postal address or phone number (unless you write to us — see section 4.6).
  • Your precise or approximate location (other than the network region your connection is routed to — see section 4.2).
  • Your voice. The App does not use the microphone.
  • Camera or passthrough images, or your likeness.
  • Hand, head, eye or controller movement history. The App uses tracking in real time to let you play and does not store it.
  • Payment details — every purchase is processed entirely by Meta.
  • Gameplay analytics, advertising identifiers, your device’s hardware identifiers, contacts, photos or files.
  • Biometric data.

4. Information the App handles, and why

4.1 Data the App collects from the Meta Horizon Platform

The App uses the Meta Horizon Platform SDK. Through it, the App collects and processes the following data, and uses it only for the purposes below:

DataPurpose
User ID — your Meta user IDTo check that you are allowed to use the App (entitlement check) and which add-ons you bought; to write your scores to the App’s leaderboards and highlight your own row; to set your presence and send and receive invites so friends can join your match.
User profile — your Meta display nameTo show your name on your fighter card in the menu and on leaderboards. Your name is not sent to other players through Photon.
Friends — your Meta friends and their online statusTo show which friends are online in the game, let you invite them or join their match, and show a friends-only leaderboard. The list is shown only to you.
Invites — the invitations you send or acceptTo invite friends to your match through Meta’s invite panel or the App’s friends list.
Deep linking — the match you were invited toWhen you accept an invite or choose “Join” on a friend in Meta Horizon, the App reads which room to open and takes you into your friend’s match.
Leaderboards — your score and clan tagYour club points and your place on the solo ladder (and your clan tag, if you set one) are stored on the App’s Meta leaderboards and shown to other players with your display name.
In-app purchases — your purchase records (item code and purchase ID)To unlock the club membership you bought, restore it on every launch, and complete donations (section 4.3).

This data is used only inside the App and is held by Meta’s services; it is never sent to us, to Photon or to any analytics provider, and never used for advertising, marketing or profiling. Meta processes it as an independent controller under its own terms and privacy policy.

4.2 Online matches (Photon)

Online matches run through Photon, a multiplayer network service of Exit Games, Inc. To connect you, Photon processes your device’s IP address and a random session identifier, and routes you to one of its server regions. During a match the App sends the other players in it — through Photon — only what the game needs to show you to each other: your fighter’s position, head and hand movements, hits, deaths and your number on the ladder. Your Meta user ID and display name are not sent. We do not run our own game servers and do not keep match data after the match ends.

4.3 Purchases and donations

The App is free to download. The club membership is a one-time add-on that unlocks the fighter editor and your own rooms; donations for the game’s servers are optional, repeatable add-ons. Both are bought through the Meta Horizon Store; Meta processes every payment and we never receive your payment details. The App reads your purchase records to unlock the membership and remembers it, and the total you have donated, only on your headset. Donations do not buy any gameplay advantage, are not charitable contributions, and refunds are governed by the Meta Horizon Store’s policies.

4.4 Safety and player reports

You can report another player through Meta’s reporting tool. Meta delivers the report to us in an encrypted inbox; reports may include the reported player’s identifiers, the reason and any details the reporter adds. We review reports to enforce fair play and community safety and may take action such as limiting a player’s access. Meta deletes reports from that inbox after 90 days.

4.5 Data that stays on your headset

Settings (sound, vibration), your ladder place and match count, your fighter’s look, your clan tag, the code of the last room you joined, membership and donation status, total time played, and the Meta user IDs of people you recently played with (to offer them in your friends list) — all stored in the App’s own storage on your headset. We cannot access this data.

4.6 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

9. Retention

  • Match data relayed through Photon: only for the duration of the match.
  • Leaderboard entries: until the leaderboard is reset, the App is retired, or you ask us to remove your entry.
  • Player reports: deleted by Meta after 90 days; records of enforcement actions for as long as they apply.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and up to 3 years for requests about your rights, to show we answered them.
  • Data on your headset: until you delete the App.

How: email vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “Dead Fools Club: Headshot — Privacy”. We will reply within 30 days (or the period your law requires), and may need to verify your request.
