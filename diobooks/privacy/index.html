DioBooks (Meta Quest) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the DioBooks app for Meta Quest (the “App”), developed and published by 80085games, collects and processes, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights and choices you have. It applies to the App as distributed through the Meta Horizon Store, and to communications with us about it. It does not apply to Meta, Cloudflare or any other service, each of which has its own privacy policy.

By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • DioBooks plays audiobooks and shows dioramas of the scene being read. The books, pictures and the librarian’s voice are built into the App or downloaded from our server; the App does not use your microphone or camera.
  • The App has its own currency, Paragraph Coins. You buy coin packs through the Meta Horizon Store checkout; Meta handles every payment and we never see your card or payment details (section 4.3).
  • Our small server keeps a pseudonymous account: your coin balance, the books you own, the releases you backed and the coins you earned. It never stores your Meta user ID itself (section 4.2).
  • If you suggest a book, we receive the title or author you type to check whether we may publish it (section 4.4).
  • We use no analytics or advertising services, show no ads, and never sell your personal data.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by email — see section 11.1.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email address, postal address or phone number (unless you write to us — section 4.5).
  • Your voice. The App does not use the microphone.
  • Camera or passthrough images, or your likeness.
  • Hand, head or controller movement history. The App uses them in real time (for example, to press a button with your finger) and does not store them.
  • Your location.
  • Your Meta display name, profile photo, avatar, friends list, payment details, advertising identifiers, contacts, photos or files. (The librarian in the App is a ready-made Meta avatar that ships with the App, not yours.)
  • What you listen to and for how long. Your listening position and settings stay on your headset (section 4.6).

4.3 Paragraph Coins, purchases and community releases

  • Coin packs are optional in-app purchases sold through the Meta Horizon Store checkout. Meta processes every payment as an independent controller; we receive only which pack was bought and a purchase identifier (section 4.1), never your card or payment details. Refunds are governed by the Meta Horizon Store’s policies; if a pack is refunded, its coins are removed from your balance.
  • With coins you can buy finished DioBooks or back the release of a new one. If a release does not reach its goal in time, your coins are returned to your balance.
  • When other readers buy a release you backed, a share of their coins is credited to you. These are coins in the App only: they cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to other people.

4.4 Suggesting a book

If you suggest a book, the App sends the title or author you typed to our server. The server looks it up in public catalogues (Project Gutenberg through the Gutendex service, and the Internet Archive) to check whether the book is in the public domain and whether a free recording exists. Only the text you typed is sent to these catalogues, never anything that identifies you. If you start a release, the suggestion is kept with that release.

4.5 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

4.6 Data that stays on your headset

Your listening position, display choices (for example subtitles and diorama size) and downloaded books are stored in the App’s own folder on your headset. We cannot access this data; it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Wherever you live, you have the right to: know and access the personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; receive it in a portable format; and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. How to exercise them: email vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “DioBooks — Privacy”. We will reply within 30 days (or the period your law requires), and may need to verify your request. If we decline a request, we will tell you why and how to appeal; you can appeal by replying to our answer.

11.1 How to request deletion of your data

  • By email: write to vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “DioBooks — Delete my data”. We will delete the coin account held under your pseudonymous key and confirm by email within 30 days. Because we do not store your Meta user ID, we may ask you to open the App once so that we can match your request to your key. Deleting your account also deletes your coin balance and the books you bought, and cannot be undone.
  • On your headset: uninstalling the App deletes everything the App stored there.
  • Data held by Meta is managed through your Meta account settings and Meta’s privacy tools.

Pseudonymous data (GDPR Art. 11): because our server keeps only a pseudonymous key, we may be unable to identify which records are yours unless you help us match your request (section 11.1).

No sale or sharing: we do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App or provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

13. Security

All connections between the App, our server and Meta are encrypted. Our server’s keys never leave the server and are never built into the App. We keep the personal data we handle to the minimum described above. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the competent authorities where the law requires.
