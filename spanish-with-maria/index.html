Spanish with Maria

Sit across the table from María, your Spanish teacher in VR on Meta Quest. She talks with you by voice, shows each phrase on a card in front of you, and lets you practise with your hands — quizzes, words from scattered letters, pairs. Free level test, free first lessons, an 18+ street-Spanish pack. Adults only (18+).
