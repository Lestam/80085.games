Wet Bottom — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Wet Bottom game for Meta Quest (the “App”), developed and published by 80085games, collects and processes, why, who receives it, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • We do not collect any personal information on our own servers — we have none. The App has no analytics, no ads and no accounts of its own.
  • Online play: when you create or join a room to play a friend (on Meta Quest or iPhone), the App connects to Photon by Exit Games. Only the battle travels — the 4-digit room code and the moves. No name, account or Meta ID is sent through Photon.
  • The microphone is used only while you hold the in-game radio key, to recognise the cell you call out (for example “delta six, fire”). Speech is recognised entirely on your headset by an offline model; no audio and no text leaves the device, and nothing is recorded or kept.
  • The App uses Meta’s platform services: Meta checks that you own the App, records an achievement, keeps the “Victories” leaderboard (your Horizon username and number of victories are shown to other players), and lets you invite friends and join them. This data stays with Meta’s services.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by email — see section 9.1.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, postal address or phone number.
  • Your location.
  • Your voice or any recording of it, camera or passthrough images, or your likeness.
  • Hand, head and controller movement: the App uses it in real time to let you play, and does not store or send it anywhere.
  • Usage statistics or analytics of any kind.
  • Payment details — the purchase is processed entirely by Meta.

4. Data the App processes, and why

4.1 Meta Horizon platform services

The App uses the Meta Horizon Platform SDK. Through it, the App receives the following data from Meta’s platform and uses it only for the purposes below:

DataWhy the App uses it
User ID — your Meta user IDTo give you your game: Meta checks that you own the App (entitlement check). To unlock the “first victory” achievement on your profile. To write your number of victories to the “Victories” leaderboard and to mark your own row in it. To set your presence while your room waits for a friend and to send and receive invites.
User profile — your Horizon usernameShown next to your score in the in-game leaderboard (world and friends tables), so players can recognise themselves and their friends. We do not use your profile photo.
Friends listUsed by Meta for the friends-only leaderboard and for Meta’s invite panel. The App does not store it.
Deep links / invites — the room you were invited toWhen you accept an invite or choose “Join” on a friend in Meta Horizon, the App reads which room to open and takes you straight into your friend’s battle.

This data is used only inside the App and is held by Meta’s services; it is never sent to us, and it is not sent to Photon. Your leaderboard entry (username and number of victories) is visible to other players of the App. We never use any of it for advertising, marketing or profiling. Meta processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

4.1a Online play (Photon)

When you create or join a room, the App connects to Photon Realtime, a multiplayer service run by Exit Games (servers in the EU region). Through it the App sends only the battle: the 4-digit room code and your moves (shots, launched planes and submarines, a rematch). Nothing that identifies you — no name, no account, no Meta ID — is sent. Like any internet service, Photon receives your device’s IP address in order to connect it; Exit Games processes it under its privacy policy. We do not store anything from these sessions.

4.2 Microphone (voice orders)

When you choose the radio controls and hold the radio key, the App listens to the microphone and recognises a small set of words — column letters, row numbers and “fire” — with an offline speech model built into the App (Vosk). Recognition happens on the headset. The audio is processed in memory and discarded at once; it is never recorded, stored or transmitted. The microphone is not used at any other time. You can refuse or withdraw the microphone permission in your headset’s settings; all other controls keep working.

4.3 Purchases

The game is sold through the Meta Horizon Store, and Meta processes every payment. We never see your payment details.

4.4 Data that stays on your headset

  • Game progress — your coins, bought planes, submarines and AA guns, the opponent’s rank and your number of victories.
  • Settings — controls, rules, backdrop, field tilt and the navigator’s voice.
  • The offline speech model, unpacked on first use of the radio.

This data is stored in the App’s own folder and is never sent to us. If Cloud Backup is turned on in your headset’s settings, Meta may back up the App’s folder to your Meta account; that backup is run by Meta and we have no access to it.

5. What we never do with data

We never sell data, never share it, never use it for advertising or marketing, and show no ads.

6. Service providers and other parties

  • Meta Platforms runs the store, the purchase, the entitlement check, achievements, the leaderboard, presence and invites under its own privacy policy.
  • Exit Games (Photon Realtime) carries online battles — room code and moves only — under its own privacy policy.

The offline speech model (Vosk, by Alpha Cephei) runs inside the App and receives nothing.

7. Retention

We hold no data about you. Data on your headset is kept until you uninstall the App. Online battles are not stored. Your leaderboard entry is kept by Meta until it is removed; Meta keeps its other platform data under its own policy.

8. Security

Nothing leaves your headset except Meta’s own platform calls, over Meta’s secure connections, and the moves of an online battle, sent through Photon.

9. Your rights

Every player, wherever they live, may ask us to access, correct or delete their data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the EEA/UK: your local data protection authority).

9.1 How to request deletion of your data

  • On your headset: uninstalling the App erases everything it stored.
  • By email: write to vnmtsl@gmail.com with the subject “Wet Bottom — Delete my data”. We will answer and confirm within 30 days. Ask us in the same email to remove your entry from the “Victories” leaderboard and we will. Data held by Meta (your achievement) can also be managed in your Meta account settings.

This right is available to every player, regardless of location.

10. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
