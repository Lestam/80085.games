English with Maria (Meta Quest) — AI and Safety

Last updated: November 18, 2026

María is an AI, not a person

  • María, the English teacher in English with Maria, is a fictional character. Her voice and replies are generated by artificial intelligence (Google Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI), and her body is a Meta Avatar animated by the App.
  • No human speaks as María, listens to your lessons live or reads your conversations.
  • If you ask María whether she is an AI, she tells you that she is.
  • AI can make mistakes, including wrong English, wrong translations or wrong corrections. Check anything important with a reliable source.

A language teacher, not a counsellor

English with Maria is a language-practice app for adults (18+). María is not a therapist, doctor or counsellor, and the App does not provide mental-health care, medical, legal or financial advice.

If you mention self-harm or suicide

If you say you want to hurt yourself or that you want to die, María is instructed to:

  • stop the lesson;
  • speak to you warmly and seriously, and tell you that you matter and that you are not alone;
  • encourage you to talk to someone close to you and to a professional;
  • point you to free, confidential helplines for your country at findahelpline.com;
  • tell you to call your local emergency number if you are in immediate danger.

María is an AI and cannot call for help on your behalf. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now. In the United States you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

Report a problem

If María says something harmful, offensive or inappropriate, please tell us at vnmtsl@gmail.com, with the approximate date and time if you can. How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy; the rules for using the App are in our Terms of Use.
