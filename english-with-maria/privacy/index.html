English with Maria (Meta Quest) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the English with Maria app for Meta Quest (the “App”), developed and published by 80085games, collects and processes, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights and choices you have. It applies to the App as distributed through the Meta Horizon Store, and to communications with us about it. It does not apply to Meta, Google, Cloudflare or any other service, each of which has its own privacy policy.

1. Summary (plain language)

  • English with Maria is a voice lesson with an AI English teacher, María. Her voice and replies are generated by Google’s Gemini models on Google Cloud (Vertex AI). When you talk to her, your voice is streamed live through our relay to Google so the AI can understand and answer you; the relay passes it on and stores nothing of it (sections 4.1 and 4.3).
  • Your learning progress — finished lessons, review schedule, level, XP and streak — is kept only on your headset (section 4.5).
  • Our small session server checks with Meta that the App is genuine and gives the headset a short-lived ticket for the voice relay. It never receives your voice and keeps no profile of you (section 4.3).
  • Payments for lesson packs are handled entirely by Meta. We never see your card or payment details (section 4.4).
  • We do not use analytics or advertising services, show no ads, and never sell your personal data.
  • The App is for adults (18+). Contact us any time to ask about or delete your data (section 12).

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your email address, postal address or phone number (unless you write to us — section 4.6). If you tell María your name, it is used only in the conversation; we never receive it.
  • Recordings of your voice. Neither we nor the App store audio recordings or transcripts of your lessons.
  • Your location, camera or passthrough images, or your likeness.
  • Hand, head or controller movement history. The App uses them in real time (for example, to let you touch an answer tile) and does not store them.
  • Your Meta display name, profile photo, avatar, friends list, payment details, advertising identifiers, contacts, photos or files.
  • Biometric data. We do not create voiceprints, face templates or any other biometric identifiers.

4. Information we collect and process, and why

4.1 Talking to María (Google Gemini)

  • María listens through your headset’s microphone, only after you grant the App the microphone permission. You can withdraw it at any time in your headset’s settings; without it María cannot hear you.
  • During a lesson the App streams your voice from your headset, through our relay on Google Cloud (section 4.3), to Vertex AI, Google’s service that runs the Gemini models, together with the lesson the App chose (the phrases to teach) and short notes from the App (for example, which answer tile you touched, your result in the level test). Gemini turns your speech into text, generates María’s answer and speaks it. The App also receives a text transcript, which it uses only in the moment and does not store.
  • Google processes this data as our processor, under the Google Cloud terms and Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Under those terms Google does not use your conversations to train or fine-tune AI models without our permission (we have given none). Google may keep data briefly in a cache and may log prompts to detect abuse, under its terms. See Google’s Vertex AI data governance, Cloud Data Processing Addendum and Privacy Policy.
  • Please do not tell María sensitive information — about your health, finances or other people. Any name you like to be called is fine.

4.3 Our servers: session server (Cloudflare) and voice relay (Google Cloud)

The App connects to two small servers we run. The session server on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.) never receives your voice or your lessons. The voice relay on Google Cloud Run passes your lesson between your headset and Vertex AI and does not store or read it; it logs only a random session number, how long the session lasted and why it ended.

4.4 Lesson packs and payments

The level test, the first lessons and practice of the lessons you finished are free. Further lesson packs and the 18+ pack are optional in-app purchases, sold through the Meta Horizon Store checkout. Meta processes every payment as an independent controller; we receive only which pack was bought and a purchase identifier, never your card or payment details. Refunds are governed by the Meta Horizon Store’s policies.

4.5 Data that stays on your headset (your progress)

So that María knows where you are in the course, the App keeps on your headset: your confirmation that you are 18 or over; the lessons you finished and your level-test result; a review schedule for the phrases you learned; your XP, level, streak and badges; and a cached list of the packs you own. This data is stored only in the App’s own folder. We cannot access it. The App does not allow this folder to be backed up to your Meta account, so it stays on the headset and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4.6 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

9. Automated processing and AI

María is an AI character: her words are generated automatically by Gemini. Her level-test result and corrections are educational guidance, not a certified language assessment, and the App makes no decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects. AI-generated answers can be wrong.

10. International data transfers

Google and Cloudflare operate worldwide, and your data may be processed in the United States and other countries. Transfers from the EEA, UK or Switzerland rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers’ data processing terms.

  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and up to 3 years for requests about your rights.

12.1 How to delete your data

  • On your headset: uninstalling the App deletes your progress and everything else it stored there.
  • On our server: we keep no data tied to you beyond the daily counter, which expires within 3 days; email us and we will confirm.
  • Data held by Google about your use of the AI service is deleted under Google’s terms; data held by Meta is managed through your Meta account.

No sale or sharing: we do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and have not done so in the past 12 months.

13. Adults only

The App is intended only for adults aged 18 or over and asks you to confirm it on first launch. It includes an optional pack of street English with slang and swear words. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has used the App, contact us.

14. Security

All connections between the App, our server, Google and Meta are encrypted. Our Google Cloud credentials never leave our servers; the headset receives only a short-lived ticket. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the competent authorities where the law requires.
